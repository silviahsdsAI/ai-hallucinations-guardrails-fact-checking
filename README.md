# AI Hallucinations, Guardrails & Fact-Checking

### AI Evaluation | Responsible AI | Quality Assurance

An educational project focused on understanding AI hallucinations, safety guardrails, fact-checking, and responsible AI evaluation.

Developed during my studies in Generative AI through the Bradesco Bootcamp on the DIO platform.

---

## 1. Project Overview

Generative AI systems can produce inaccurate, misleading, or unsupported information.

This project explores the importance of identifying AI hallucinations, understanding safety mechanisms, and evaluating AI-generated responses.

The project consists of five educational study guides covering theoretical concepts, technical terminology, practical scenarios, review questions, and final reflections.

## 2. Project Objectives

- Understand AI hallucinations and their potential risks.
- Explore AI guardrails and safety mechanisms.
- Study fact-checking and source verification.
- Understand prompt injection and related security risks.
- Develop analytical thinking for AI response evaluation.
- Strengthen knowledge of Responsible AI principles.

## 3. Project Development

The educational project was organized into five study guides.

### Part 1 — Fundamentals

Study of AI hallucinations, their possible causes, practical examples, and their relationship with guardrails and fact-checking.

Topics include:

- AI hallucinations.
- Safety guardrails.
- Fact-checking.
- Relationships between the three concepts.

### Part 2 — Technical Glossary

Development of a technical glossary explaining terminology related to Generative AI and AI safety.

Topics include:

- Application Programming Interface (API).
- AI Hallucinations.
- AI Guardrails.
- Input Filters.
- Action Guards.
- Other concepts related to AI evaluation and safety.

### Part 3 — Practical Scenarios

Exploration of educational scenarios involving AI security risks and mitigation strategies.

Topics include:

- Prompt injection.
- Malicious instructions.
- Input filtering.
- Security controls.
- Responsible AI behavior.

These scenarios were studied as educational examples rather than implemented as production systems.

### Part 4 — Review Questions

Creation of an educational review guide containing five multiple-choice questions with explanations.

Topics include:

- AI hallucination identification.
- AI safety mechanisms.
- Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).
- Fact-checking.
- Human oversight.

### Part 5 — Final Synthesis and Learning Outcomes

Consolidation of the main concepts studied throughout the project.

The final synthesis explores the relationship between AI hallucinations, guardrails, fact-checking, RAG, and human supervision.

It also reflects on the importance of responsible AI development and evaluation.

## 4. AI Evaluation Workflow

A basic educational AI response evaluation workflow may include:

1. Read the AI-generated response.
2. Identify factual claims.
3. Locate relevant and reliable sources.
4. Compare the claims with available evidence.
5. Identify unsupported or misleading information.
6. Document the evaluation findings.

This workflow represents concepts studied during the project.

## 5. Practical Evaluation Example

### AI-generated claim

"Every AI-generated response is factually accurate."

### Evaluation

Unsupported claim.

### Explanation

Generative AI systems can produce incorrect, misleading, or fabricated information.

Therefore, AI-generated responses should not automatically be considered factually accurate.

### Recommended Action

Flag the claim and verify the information using reliable sources.
